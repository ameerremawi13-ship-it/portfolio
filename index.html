Workplace Interior Design

Al Safa Bank

Bank & Café Hybrid Concept

The concept of our project is based on integrating customer satisfaction with the creation of comfortable and welcoming spaces for both customers and employees. Rather than functioning solely as a traditional banking environment, the space is designed as a hybrid experience that combines the efficiency of banking services with the atmosphere of a café — providing areas for relaxation, social interaction and informal work, and creating a more user-centered environment that enhances the overall experience for both customers and staff.

Full interior design by Ameer Halabi

Residential Retreat Design

Mountain House

Ramallah, Palestine

A remote, quiet mountain house in Ramallah with a small room and an outdoor courtyard centered on a large water fountain, plus an outdoor seating area. I selected all the materials and placed all the furniture, designed the courtyard fountain and the outdoor seating area, and developed the complete design — including replacing a solid wall with a full-length glass wall that opens along its entire length.

Interior & courtyard design by Ameer Halabi
